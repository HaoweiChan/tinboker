## Metric review: is 討論佔比 the right number? (2026-09-10, analysis4.py, results4.json)

15 variants on one weekly panel (2025-08+ whole market; 2020-25 for 124 tickers). Three findings:

1. **Every "÷ whole-corpus" variant (current V1, same-market V2, episode rate V3, show rate V4, 4-week count share V5, depth-weighted V6, show-equal-weighted V7, any half-life) is corpus-size dependent.** Weekly mentions went 68 → 236 across 2025-08; the median mentioned ticker's share fell to 15–27% of its prior level because more tickers get mentioned. **Cause is the roster, not the extractor** (checked 2026-09-10 after peer question): 兆華與股惑仔 first airs in the corpus 2025-07 (17–19 episodes/month, ~16–20 insights per episode, the densest show) and 財經一路發 2025-08 (37–47 episodes/month, ~10 per episode); incumbents' per-episode density is unchanged (財女珍妮 4–6, M觀點 6–7, 游庭皓 3–4). schema_version 3 coexists with 2 from 2025-04 on, no step. All insights for 2025-05..09 carry created_at in 2026-05..09, i.e. they were (re)generated by the current extractor regardless of air date — provenance is uniform. The earlier 'same 5 active shows' line was wrong: the median count of distinct shows per week is 5 on both sides, but the set changed. Consequence for 誰講對了: per-show sample sizes are dominated by 兆華 and 一路發 from those months, a roster effect to footnote, not a break. Spearman with weekly N: −0.6 to −0.9. Only own-history variants (V8 ratio-to-8w, V9 z26, V10 52-week percentile) are stable (post/pre ratio 0.8–1.3, corr −0.2 to −0.5). Any fixed share threshold (incl. my 1% decay threshold) drifts. V4 show_rate is the least sensitive level metric (ratio 0.60).
2. **Three families, three signals.** Level family (V1–V7) inter-correlate 0.65–0.98 and have positive IC on xf60 (+0.11 to +0.18, 2025-08+; +0.05 to +0.08 in 2020-25) = megacap/AI beta; longer half-life scores higher (hl28 IC 0.181 vs hl3 0.113). Own-history family (V8–V10) has NEGATIVE IC: −0.14 to −0.16 on xf60 (t −6 to −9), −0.05 to −0.08 on xf20 (t −2.8 to −3.9), and −0.06 to −0.08 in 2020-25 (t −2.5 to −3.6): among mentioned tickers, those at their own attention high (Q5) return −1 to +1% x60 vs +9–10% for those at their own low (Q1). Cross-era contrarian signal, no threshold needed; supersedes the binary decay state. V11 attention-vs-money (TW, log(mention share ÷ trading-value share)) is the strongest single variant: IC −0.18 on xf60 (t −8.4), −0.08 on xf20 (t −4.4), Q5 −7.4% vs Q1 +11.4%; ≈0 correlation with the level family, 0.35–0.40 with own-history. TW 2025-08+ only.
3. Persistence: level family 4-week rank autocorr 0.6–0.9; own_ratio −0.59 (mean-reverting by construction); z26 0.14; 52w percentile 0.03.

Recommendation for the product metric: keep a level number only as "how big is this ticker in podcast discourse" (V4 show_rate reads best: "5 of 7 shows in 4 weeks"); express heat as own-history percentile (V10) or z26 (V9); add attention ÷ trading-value share for TW; half-life is a display/UX choice (7d reads as 'this week'); the 14–28d IC advantage belongs to the research panel and is not a reason to change the page formula (peer objection accepted). Caveat: 60d IC t-stats are inflated by overlapping windows; the 20d column is the honest one and carries the same signs.

## Channel-frequency normalization (2026-09-11, analysis5.py, results5.json)

Willy's core concern: a mention from a daily 10-ticker show (財經一路發) should count less than one from a twice-weekly show. Tested V12 efficiency-weighted share (each mention ÷ that show's trailing-8-week mention volume, summed over 4 weeks, ÷ all tickers), V12b = V12's own 52-week percentile, V13 = per-show own-history z of the ticker's within-show share, averaged over shows. Results (2025-08+, mentioned): V12 IC +0.158 x60 (vs V1 +0.148), corr 0.83 with V1 / 0.90 with V7, corpus ratio 0.08 — same level family, same beta, same drift. V12b IC −0.132 (V10 −0.139), corr 0.75 with V10, 2020-25 −0.04 (V10 −0.063). V13 IC −0.130, corpus ratio 2.47 (unstable when new shows enter with short histories). Channel-frequency weighting adds no information beyond comparing a ticker to its own history; V10 (52-week own percentile of the plain share) remains the recommended heat field. V4 (one vote per show) and V7 (equal-weighted shows) were already channel-normalized level metrics and also did not beat V1.
